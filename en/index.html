AREAS OF PRACTICE

Autism treatment and speech, behavioral, and socialization disorders

Work with children with autism spectrum disorders (ASD), speech delays, and psycho-emotional developmental delays....

Methods: comprehensive neurological and psychological diagnostics, rehabilitation using physical and mental rehabilitation methods, psychocorrection.

Goal: improvement of speech skills, social adaptation, and reduction of behavioral disorders.

Cerebral Palsy (CP)

Many years of experience working with children with cerebral palsy....

Methods: neurological supervision, physical rehabilitation, psycho-emotional correction, application of international rehabilitation protocols.

Goal: development of motor skills, adaptation to daily life, and improvement of quality of life.

Herniated and protruding intervertebral discs

Comprehensive care for patients with back pain, migraines, mobility impairments, and consequences of injuries and strokes....

Methods: diagnostics, neurological treatment, rehabilitation programs (spinal correction, massage, kinesitherapy, physiotherapy, individualized exercises).

Goal: pain relief, restoration of spinal function, and prevention of complications.

Other neurological conditions

Care for a wide range of neurological disorders in children and adults....

Areas: headaches, epilepsy, sequelae of strokes and injuries, stress-related disorders, developmental delays.

Goal: improvement of quality of life and the maximum possible restoration of functions.

EEG
(Electroencephalography)

A diagnostic test of the brain's bioelectrical activity in children and adults. Used to assess the functional state of the brain and detect abnormalities, including epilepsy....

Methods: EEG recording at rest and with functional tests, interpretation by a neurologist, dynamic follow-up.

Goal: early detection of epileptic activity, assessment of brain structure maturity, refinement of the diagnosis, and monitoring of treatment effectiveness.

ABOUT ME

I specialize in the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of neurological disorders in both children and adults. Since 2009, I have worked in international rehabilitation clinics of the Kozyavkin Group (Ukraine, Cyprus, UAE, Saudi Arabia), as well as at the Department of Physical Therapy, Rehabilitation and Occupational Therapy at DDPU. My goal is to help patients restore their health and psychological well-being through a comprehensive approach: neurology, psychology, and rehabilitation.

MY APPROACH

I have developed my own treatment philosophy and believe that true neurology is not a set of isolated procedures, but a comprehensive interdisciplinary approach to restoring the functions of the nervous system and the psyche.

Therefore, I:

  • View the central nervous system, the musculoskeletal system, and the psycho-emotional state as a single interconnected system;
  • Apply the principles of neuroplasticity to restore brain function and behavior;
  • Implement international protocols in pediatric and adult neurology, medical psychology, and neurorehabilitation;
  • Work with both acute conditions (strokes, injuries, neurological crises) and chronic disorders (ASD, cerebral palsy, intervertebral disc herniations, cognitive impairments).
